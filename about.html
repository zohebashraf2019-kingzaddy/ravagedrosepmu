Hi, I'm Destiny

The artist behind Ravaged Rose Beauty

I believe beauty isn't about becoming someone else. It's about rediscovering the confidence that's been there all along.

Ravaged Rose Beauty is a permanent makeup studio born from resilience, reinvention and a passion for helping women feel beautiful in their own skin. To me, every face tells a story — and no two stories are alike. That's why my approach is personal, intentional and never one-size-fits-all.

From beautifully defined brows to effortlessly enhanced lips, my goal is to create results that feel like you, only more confident.
