Top questions before you book

01How long does PMU last?

Results depend on your skin type, lifestyle, sun exposure, skincare routine, and how your skin retains pigment.

  • Powder Brows: Typically maintain their appearance for 1–3 years before a color refresh may be desired.
  • Lip Blush: Typically maintains its appearance for 2–3 years, sometimes longer.

These are general estimates. Individual results and pigment retention vary.

02Does it hurt?

Most clients experience mild to moderate discomfort rather than significant pain. Sensitivity varies depending on the treatment area and individual pain tolerance.

When appropriate, approved topical anesthetics may be used in accordance with applicable instructions and regulations to help improve comfort.

03How long does healing take?

Powder Brows: Initial surface healing generally takes 7–14 days. Complete skin recovery and pigment stabilization may take approximately 4–8 weeks. Healing timelines vary for each client.

Lip Blush: Initial surface healing generally takes approximately 5–10 days. Your lips may initially appear brighter, followed by temporary lightening or uneven color. The final appearance usually becomes easier to evaluate after approximately 6–8 weeks.

Healing timelines vary for each client — always follow the aftercare instructions provided for your treatment.

04Will I need a touch-up?

Some clients benefit from a perfecting session to refine areas that healed lighter or unevenly.

This is commonly considered approximately 6–8 weeks after the initial treatment, once the skin has healed sufficiently.

Additional appointments and fees depend on your selected service.

Powder brows and lip blush include your 6-week perfecting session — no additional service charge, just a $100 tray fee for the products and disposable supplies used. Existing clients also receive a discounted touch-up rate within 2 years of their original service.

05What's your cancellation policy?

A nonrefundable booking fee is required to reserve your appointment and is applied toward your service total.

  • 48-Hour Notice: All cancellations and rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before your scheduled appointment.
  • One Complimentary Reschedule: Your booking fee can be transferred to one rescheduled appointment when at least 48 hours' notice is provided.
  • Late Cancellations & No-Shows: Cancellations or reschedules with less than 48 hours' notice, and missed appointments, forfeit the booking fee.
  • Additional Rescheduling: After your one complimentary reschedule, further changes require a new booking fee.

See our full Booking & Studio Policies for details and applicable exceptions.

✦General Permanent Makeup Questions6 questions
What is permanent makeup (PMU)?

Permanent makeup is a cosmetic tattooing technique that enhances your natural features by carefully depositing pigment into the skin. Popular treatments include powder brows and lip blush.

Although commonly referred to as semi-permanent makeup, the pigment can remain in your skin indefinitely. Its visible appearance typically softens and fades over time.

How long does an appointment take?

Most initial permanent makeup appointments take approximately 2–3 hours.

Your appointment includes consultation, facial mapping or design, pigment selection, and the procedure itself. Corrective work and dark lip neutralization may require additional time.

Is permanent makeup safe?

Your health and safety are our priorities.

At Ravaged Rose PMU, we follow applicable sanitation and infection-control requirements, including appropriate single-use supplies and sterilization practices.

Because permanent makeup involves tattooing the skin, potential risks include infection, allergic reactions, scarring, and pigment changes. Every client must complete the required health questionnaire and consent documentation before treatment.

Can anyone receive permanent makeup?

Not everyone is a suitable candidate.

Certain health conditions, medications, skin concerns, recent cosmetic treatments, pregnancy, and other factors may affect eligibility.

Please complete your intake questionnaire accurately so we can evaluate whether treatment is appropriate for you.

Can I receive permanent makeup while pregnant or breastfeeding?

We do not perform elective permanent makeup procedures during pregnancy.

If you're breastfeeding, please contact us before booking so we can discuss treatment timing, potential risks, and whether medical guidance is appropriate.

Do I need a consultation?

Some services require a consultation, especially corrective brows, previously tattooed areas, dark lip neutralization, and removal procedures.

Consultations allow us to evaluate your skin, understand your goals, and recommend a realistic treatment plan.

〰Powder Brows7 questions
What are powder brows?

Powder brows are a cosmetic tattoo technique that creates a soft, shaded effect similar to perfectly applied eyebrow makeup.

Whether you prefer a natural, subtle finish or a more defined appearance, the treatment can be customized to complement your facial features and skin characteristics.

Will my eyebrows look too dark immediately afterward?

Freshly treated brows typically appear darker and more defined than the final healed result.

As your skin heals, the color gradually softens. Temporary unevenness and changes in appearance are normal throughout healing.

Are powder brows suitable for oily skin?

Yes! Powder brows are often an excellent option for oily or combination skin.

However, natural oil production can influence healing, pigment retention, and long-term appearance.

Will I need to shave my eyebrows?

No. Your natural eyebrow hair is generally preserved.

Minor grooming may be performed when needed to refine the shape.

Can you cover my previous eyebrow tattoo?

Possibly. It depends on the color, saturation, placement, and condition of your existing pigment.

Some previous tattoos may require lightening or removal before new pigment can be applied successfully.

A consultation is required before corrective work.

Can you correct red, pink, gray, or blue eyebrows?

Certain unwanted pigment tones can be improved through specialized color correction.

However, results depend on the existing pigment and skin condition. Multiple sessions or removal may be recommended.

How long do powder brows take to heal?

Initial surface healing generally takes 7–14 days.

Complete skin recovery and pigment stabilization may take approximately 4–8 weeks. Healing timelines vary for each client.

💋Lip Blush & Dark Lip Neutralization10 questions
What is lip blush?

Lip blush is a cosmetic tattoo treatment designed to enhance your natural lip color, improve visual definition, and create a more balanced appearance.

It's ideal for clients who want a soft, polished look without applying lipstick every day.

Will lip blush make my lips bigger?

Lip blush does not physically increase lip volume.

However, enhancing your natural lip color and borders can create the appearance of fuller, more defined lips.

It is not a substitute for lip filler, and pigment is not intentionally applied beyond the natural lip tissue to create artificial volume.

Can I choose my lip color?

Absolutely!

We'll discuss your desired appearance and help select a pigment that complements your natural lip color, skin tone, and goals.

Keep in mind that healed results may look different from freshly applied pigment.

How long does lip blush take to heal?

Initial surface healing generally takes approximately 5–10 days.

Your lips may initially appear brighter, followed by temporary lightening or uneven color.

The final appearance usually becomes easier to evaluate after approximately 6–8 weeks.

Why does my lip color seem to disappear during healing?

Temporary lightening and uneven pigmentation are common during healing.

As your skin recovers, the pigment's appearance can change, and some pigment may be lost.

Allow the recommended healing period before evaluating your final results.

Can I receive lip blush if I already have lip filler?

In many cases, yes.

However, existing filler can affect lip shape, symmetry, and tissue tension.

We recommend allowing your lips to settle completely after filler treatments. A consultation may be necessary.

Can I get lip blush if I have a history of cold sores?

Yes, potentially, but additional precautions are important.

Lip tattooing can trigger a cold sore outbreak. If you have a history of cold sores, consult your healthcare provider before treatment about whether prescription antiviral medication is appropriate.

We cannot perform lip blush on an active cold sore or infection.

What is dark lip neutralization?

Dark lip neutralization is a specialized cosmetic tattoo procedure designed to gradually balance the appearance of naturally darker or unevenly pigmented lips.

The process often requires multiple treatments to achieve the desired appearance.

How many dark lip neutralization sessions will I need?

Many clients require approximately 2–4 sessions, although some may need additional treatments.

The exact number depends on your natural pigmentation, healing response, and desired outcome.

Can lip blush correct uneven or asymmetrical lips?

Lip blush may improve the visual appearance of minor differences in lip color or borders.

However, natural anatomy, muscle movement, existing filler, and other factors can limit results. Perfect symmetry cannot be guaranteed.

🌿Healing & Aftercare7 questions
Can I wear makeup after my appointment?

Avoid applying makeup directly to the treated area until the skin has healed.

Applying cosmetics too early can introduce bacteria, cause irritation, and interfere with healing.

Can I exercise after permanent makeup?

Avoid strenuous exercise, excessive sweating, saunas, and activities that expose the treated area to significant heat or moisture during early healing.

Always follow the aftercare instructions provided for your treatment.

Can I swim after my appointment?

Avoid swimming pools, hot tubs, lakes, and soaking activities until the treated area has healed.

Premature exposure may increase the risk of irritation or infection.

Can I go into the sun after permanent makeup?

Freshly treated skin should be protected from direct sunlight and tanning.

Once fully healed, use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher on exposed treated skin to help reduce UV-related fading and skin damage.

For lip treatments, choose an SPF lip balm.

Can I pick at peeling skin or scabs?

No. Allow peeling and flaking to occur naturally.

Picking can disrupt healing and increase the risk of pigment loss, irritation, and scarring.

Which skincare products should I avoid?

Avoid applying retinoids, exfoliating acids, chemical peels, and other irritating active ingredients directly to the healing area.

Certain skincare ingredients may also affect the tattoo's appearance over time.

What should I do if I suspect an infection?

Contact a healthcare professional promptly if you experience spreading redness, increasing pain, warmth, significant swelling, pus, fever, or other concerning symptoms.

Do not rely on cosmetic aftercare products to treat a suspected infection.

✧Tiny Tattoos3 questions
Are tiny tattoos permanent?

Yes! Tiny tattoos are permanent body art.

Although their appearance may soften or fade over time, pigment can remain in the skin indefinitely.

Will my tiny tattoo always look as delicate as it does when fresh?

Not necessarily.

Fine lines may soften or spread slightly over time as the skin changes and pigment settles.

Extremely small details may become less distinct, which is why we consider design size, placement, and long-term appearance.

Can I bring my own tattoo design?

Absolutely!

You're welcome to bring inspiration photos, personal artwork, or creative ideas.

Some designs may need adjustments to ensure they work well with your desired size, placement, and long-term appearance.

Brow + lip aftercare

Heal beautifully

Destiny will confirm any product-specific directions at your appointment — always follow those first.

✦

Both services

Keep the area clean. Wash hands before touching it. Follow the cleansing and ointment instructions provided by your artist. Do not pick, peel, scratch, or exfoliate healing skin. Avoid swimming, hot tubs, saunas, heavy sweating, and makeup directly on the healing area until healed. Avoid direct sun exposure and tanning during healing.

〰

Brows

Avoid brow makeup, exfoliants, retinoids, acids, and facial treatments over the area while healing. Do not rub or remove flaking skin.

💋

Lips

Keep lips moisturized with the recommended product. Avoid irritating, spicy, acidic, or very hot foods if they cause discomfort. Do not pick flakes. If you have a history of cold sores, follow the plan discussed with your healthcare professional.

☀

After healing

Protect the treated area from sun exposure. Once fully healed, apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher to brows and use SPF lip balm on lips, reapplying as directed. UV exposure can accelerate pigment fading.

When to get help: Seek medical advice promptly for spreading redness, worsening pain or swelling, pus, fever, or other signs of infection. Seek emergency care for severe allergic symptoms.
