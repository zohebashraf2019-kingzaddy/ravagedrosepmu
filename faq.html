Frequently asked

Questions

What is the 6-week perfecting session?

Beautiful brows and lips are a process! 6–8 weeks after your initial appointment we assess your healed results, refine the shape, fill in areas where pigment healed lighter and adjust color or definition. For powder brows and lip blush there's no additional service charge — just a $100 tray fee for the products and disposable supplies used.

How long does permanent makeup last?

Results naturally soften over time depending on your skin, lifestyle and sun exposure. That's why an annual touch up is recommended to refresh color — existing clients get a discounted touch-up rate within 2 years of their original service.

Do I need a deposit?

Yes — new clients place a deposit to secure their appointment: $50 for powder brows, lip blush, dark lip neutralization and saline removal, and $20 for tiny tattoos. Deposits are applied toward your service total.

Do I need a consultation?

A consultation is required for Dark Lip Neutralization ($35, applied to your final balance) and Saline & Glycolic Removal. Tiny tattoos may require a consultation depending on the design.

Can I use Affirm, Afterpay or Klarna?

Yes, through the booking site for bookings between $50 and $4,000. Pre-approval is required before your appointment, payment is processed before your service begins, and promotional or discounted pricing can't be combined with buy now, pay later.

How many sessions does Dark Lip Neutralization take?

Typically 2–4 sessions, depending on your natural pigmentation and healing. The initial appointment is $400, and each additional appointment in the same treatment program is $200.

Can you fix or remove old permanent makeup?

Saline and glycolic removal can help lift and lighten unwanted or poorly healed pigment. Laser removal is typically recommended as a first step to reduce existing pigment. A consultation is required, multiple sessions may be needed and results vary.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes! Gift cards are available through the booking site — the perfect way to treat someone (or yourself).

Prepare & heal

General guidance

Destiny will give you personalized pre- and aftercare instructions at your appointment — always follow those first.

✦

Before your visit

  • Come with a clean, makeup-free treatment area if you can.
  • Let us know about any skin conditions, medications or prior PMU during your consultation.
  • If using buy now, pay later, get pre-approved beforehand.
🌹

While you heal

  • Expect color to look bolder at first, then soften as it heals.
  • Keep the area clean and follow your aftercare sheet exactly.
  • Avoid picking or scratching while it heals.
💗

Your perfecting session

  • Book it for 6–8 weeks after your first appointment.
  • This is where shape and color are refined to their final look.
  • Annual touch ups keep everything fresh.
