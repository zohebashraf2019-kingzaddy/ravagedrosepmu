Client forms

Prepared for your appointment

Every client completes the forms for their service before treatment. Fill out and sign your forms online in a few minutes — they go straight to Destiny. Prefer paper? Every form is also available as a printable PDF.

✦

Incomplete documentation may delay or reschedule your appointment. Please answer honestly, bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and tell Destiny if anything changes before your visit. Questions? Call or text (775) 342-4443.

Brow + Lip Aftercare

Aftercare instructions and acknowledgment for brow PMU and lip blush.

Covers
  • Keep the area clean and don't pick, peel or scratch.
