Booking & Studio PoliciesTerms & ConditionsPrivacy Policy

At Ravaged Rose PMU, we are committed to providing every client with a personalized, professional, and exceptional beauty experience. Each appointment is carefully reserved to allow the time, attention, and precision your service deserves.

To ensure a smooth booking process and maintain the highest standards of safety and service, we kindly ask all clients to review and understand the following policies before scheduling an appointment.

1Booking Fees & Payments

A nonrefundable booking fee is required to reserve your appointment. This fee will be applied toward the total cost of your selected service, with the remaining balance due at your appointment.

  • Appointments are not confirmed until the required booking fee has been received and your booking has been accepted.
  • Booking fees are nonrefundable except where required by applicable law or if Ravaged Rose PMU cancels an appointment and cannot offer an acceptable alternative.
  • Booking fees cannot be transferred to another individual.
  • The remaining balance must be paid before leaving the studio.

2Cancellations & Rescheduling

We understand that unexpected circumstances may arise. To respect both your time and ours, the following cancellation and rescheduling policies apply:

  • 48-Hour Notice: All cancellations and rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before your scheduled appointment.
  • One Complimentary Reschedule: Clients may transfer their original booking fee to one rescheduled appointment when at least 48 hours' notice is provided.
  • Late Cancellations: Cancellations or rescheduling requests made less than 48 hours before an appointment will result in forfeiture of the booking fee.
  • Additional Rescheduling: After the one complimentary reschedule, any further appointment changes will require a new booking fee.
  • No-Shows: Clients who do not attend their scheduled appointment without providing notice will forfeit their booking fee.

All rescheduled appointments are subject to availability. A new booking fee is required when the original fee has been forfeited.

3Late Arrival Policy

We kindly ask that you arrive on time for your scheduled appointment to ensure your service can be performed safely and without interruption.

Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be required to reschedule if there is insufficient time to complete the procedure properly.

If your appointment cannot be accommodated due to late arrival, your booking fee may be forfeited, and a new fee may be required to secure another appointment.

4Consultations & Service Approval

Certain permanent makeup and cosmetic procedures require a consultation before treatment can be approved.

These services may include:

  • Corrective permanent makeup
  • Previously tattooed or treated eyebrows
  • Dark lip neutralization
  • Permanent makeup or tattoo removal
  • Other advanced or corrective cosmetic procedures

All required consultations must be completed and approved before the treatment appointment.

Ravaged Rose PMU reserves the right to postpone or decline a service if it is determined that the procedure may not be suitable for the client's skin condition, health, or desired results.

5Client Intake Forms & Identification

For the safety of our clients and compliance with applicable requirements, all necessary documentation must be completed before treatment.

Clients are required to:

  • Complete all applicable intake questionnaires and health disclosure forms.
  • Review and electronically sign required consent forms and liability waivers.
  • Provide a valid government-issued photo ID for identity and age verification.
  • Ensure that all submitted information is accurate, complete, and belongs to the individual receiving the service.

Clients may be asked to upload identification securely through our website and present their original photo ID upon arrival.

Incomplete documentation may result in an appointment being delayed or rescheduled.

6Health & Treatment Eligibility

Your health and safety are our highest priorities.

Before your appointment, please disclose any relevant medical conditions or circumstances that could affect your procedure, including:

  • Allergies or skin sensitivities
  • Active skin infections or irritation
  • Medications that may affect treatment or healing
  • Recent cosmetic procedures or treatments
  • Existing skin conditions or healing concerns
  • Any other health-related circumstances that may influence your results

Certain conditions may require additional evaluation, medical clearance, or postponement of treatment.

Ravaged Rose PMU reserves the right to decline or reschedule a procedure when necessary to protect client health and safety.

If you are unsure whether a service is appropriate for you, please contact us before booking.

7Results, Healing & Expectations

Permanent makeup and cosmetic tattooing are highly individualized procedures. Results may vary based on several factors, including:

  • Skin type and condition
  • Natural healing response
  • Lifestyle and skincare routines
  • Pigment retention
  • Health and medical factors
  • Compliance with aftercare instructions

While we strive to achieve beautiful, balanced, and long-lasting results, perfect symmetry, exact pigment retention, and specific healed outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Some treatments may require multiple sessions to achieve the desired appearance. Additional sessions may involve separate charges.

8Touch-Ups & Perfecting Appointments

Touch-up and perfecting appointments are not automatically included in the original service price unless specifically stated in the service description.

When a perfecting session is included:

  • The appointment must be scheduled within the specified eligibility period.
  • A disclosed supply or tray fee may apply.
  • Appointments outside the eligible timeframe may be considered a separate service.
  • Requests involving a new shape, design, color, or technique may require additional charges.

Clients are encouraged to review the details of their selected service before booking.

9Payment Options & Financing

All outstanding service balances must be paid in full before leaving the studio.

Where available, Ravaged Rose PMU may offer approved third-party financing or buy now, pay later payment options.

  • Financing approval must be obtained before the procedure begins.
  • All required payment processing must be completed before treatment.
  • Financing eligibility and approval are determined by the third-party provider.
  • Financing arrangements are subject to the provider's terms and conditions.

Ravaged Rose PMU cannot guarantee financing approval.

10Studio Safety & Guest Policy

To maintain a clean, safe, and relaxing treatment environment, we ask clients to respect the following studio guidelines:

  • Only the scheduled client is permitted in the treatment area unless prior approval has been granted.
  • Children and additional guests may be restricted from treatment areas.
  • Pets are not permitted in treatment areas, except legally recognized service animals as required by applicable law.
  • Clients must follow all reasonable sanitation and safety instructions provided by studio staff.

Ravaged Rose PMU reserves the right to discontinue, postpone, or decline a service when necessary to maintain a safe and professional environment.

11Policy Acknowledgment & Acceptance

By scheduling an appointment and submitting the required booking fee, you acknowledge that you have reviewed, understood, and agreed to the booking and studio policies outlined above, subject to applicable law.

These policies are designed to protect our clients, respect appointment availability, and maintain the quality of service that defines Ravaged Rose PMU.

We appreciate your trust and look forward to providing you with a beautiful, personalized experience.

Thank you for choosing Ravaged Rose PMU.
