Welcome to Ravaged Rose PMU

At Ravaged Rose PMU, we're dedicated to providing a safe, professional, and personalized beauty experience.

Before booking an appointment or receiving services, please review the following Terms & Conditions. These guidelines are designed to promote transparency, protect our clients, and ensure a positive experience from booking through aftercare.

By accessing our website, submitting information, or booking an appointment, you acknowledge these Terms & Conditions. During booking, you will be asked to confirm your agreement.

1Our Services

Ravaged Rose PMU specializes in cosmetic tattooing, permanent makeup, tiny tattoos, and additional beauty services listed on our website.

All services are subject to appointment availability, client eligibility, required consultations, and professional evaluation.

We reserve the right to decline, reschedule, or postpone a procedure when necessary for legitimate health, safety, legal, or professional reasons, consistent with applicable law.

Our goal is to provide services responsibly while prioritizing client well-being and satisfaction.

2Appointments & Booking Fees

A nonrefundable booking fee is required to secure your appointment.

Your booking fee is credited toward the total cost of your scheduled service.

Appointments are considered confirmed only after the required booking fee has been successfully processed and confirmation has been issued.

Cancellations, rescheduling requests, late arrivals, and missed appointments are governed by our separate Booking & Studio Policies, which form part of these Terms & Conditions.

All applicable booking fees and additional charges will be disclosed before payment.

We encourage clients to review our policies carefully before reserving an appointment.

3Pricing & Payment Options

All service prices are listed in U.S. dollars.

Pricing may vary depending on the selected procedure, treatment complexity, and individual client needs.

The price confirmed at booking will be honored for the scheduled service unless additional work or modifications are requested.

Any additional fees will be clearly explained and must be accepted by the client before additional services are performed.

When available, payment plans or financing options may be offered through participating third-party providers.

Financing eligibility, approval, and repayment terms are determined by the individual provider.

4Medical Information & Client Responsibilities

Your health and safety are essential to a successful treatment experience.

Clients are responsible for providing complete and accurate information regarding:

  • Medical history and relevant health conditions
  • Allergies and skin sensitivities
  • Current medications
  • Previous permanent makeup or cosmetic treatments
  • Any conditions that may affect treatment or healing

Failure to disclose relevant information may result in postponement or cancellation of a procedure.

Clients are also responsible for following all preparation and aftercare instructions provided for their treatment.

Following these instructions helps support proper healing and the best possible results.

5Informed Consent & Treatment Risks

Permanent makeup and tattoo procedures involve placing pigment into the skin.

As with any cosmetic tattoo procedure, potential risks may include infection, allergic reactions, scarring, pigment migration, color changes, uneven healing, and dissatisfaction with the final appearance.

Before receiving treatment, clients must review and sign the appropriate procedure- specific informed consent documents.

Accepting these website Terms & Conditions does not replace the separate informed consent required for your procedure.

We encourage clients to ask questions and discuss any concerns before treatment begins.

6Results & Individual Healing

Every client's skin, healing process, and pigment retention are unique.

Final results may vary based on factors including skin type, lifestyle, skincare routines, sun exposure, existing pigmentation, and individual healing responses.

Although we strive to provide high-quality, carefully customized results, Ravaged Rose PMU cannot guarantee:

  • An exact final pigment color
  • Perfect symmetry or a particular cosmetic appearance
  • Specific pigment retention or longevity
  • Identical results for every client
  • A guaranteed number of sessions to achieve a desired outcome

Some treatments may require additional appointments to refine or maintain results.

Nothing in this section limits any rights or remedies available to clients under applicable law.

7Corrective Procedures & Additional Treatments

Clients with previous permanent makeup, existing pigment, scar tissue, or certain skin conditions may require additional evaluation before treatment.

Corrective procedures can be more complex and may involve multiple sessions, pigment lightening, removal, or referral to another qualified professional.

Not every previous cosmetic tattoo can be successfully corrected or covered.

Any additional treatment costs will be disclosed before scheduling.

A consultation may be required to determine the safest and most appropriate treatment approach.

8Photography & Video Authorization

Photographs and videos may be taken to document treatments, monitor healing, and maintain accurate client records, subject to applicable privacy and consent requirements.

However, your permission is required before identifiable photographs or videos are used publicly for marketing or promotional purposes.

Clients may select their preferred authorization through our separate Photo & Video Release:

Full Image Authorization: Permission to use approved photographs or videos that may include identifiable facial features.

Cropped Image Authorization: Permission to use approved images limited to the treatment area without intentionally displaying the full face.

No Public Use: Photographs and videos will not be used for advertising, social media, website galleries, or other public promotions.

Choosing not to authorize promotional photography will not affect eligibility for services.

Your privacy and personal preferences will always be respected.
