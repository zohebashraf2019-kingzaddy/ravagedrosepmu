Your Privacy Matters to Us

At Ravaged Rose PMU, your privacy, safety, and trust are our priorities.

We understand that choosing permanent makeup and cosmetic tattoo services involves sharing personal and sometimes sensitive information. We are committed to treating that information with care, confidentiality, and respect.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you.

By using our website or providing personal information, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1Information We Collect

To provide a safe, personalized, and professional experience, we may collect information when you visit our website, book an appointment, complete client forms, or receive services.

Personal & Contact Information

  • Full legal name and date of birth
  • Phone number and email address
  • Residential address
  • Emergency contact information, when provided

Identification Information

  • Government-issued photo identification submitted through our secure client portal
  • Information necessary to verify your identity and age

Health & Treatment Information

  • Relevant medical history and health conditions
  • Allergies, medications, and skin sensitivities
  • Previous cosmetic treatments, tattoos, or permanent makeup
  • Intake questionnaires and signed consent forms
  • Treatment notes, appointment history, and aftercare documentation

Photography & Video

  • Before-and-after treatment photographs
  • Images documenting procedures and healing
  • Promotional photographs or videos when separately authorized

Booking & Payment Information

  • Appointment details and booking history
  • Booking fees and transaction records
  • Limited payment information provided by payment processors

Payment card details may be processed directly through third-party payment providers rather than stored by Ravaged Rose PMU.

2How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide a safe, organized, and personalized client experience.

This includes:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and managing appointments
  • Verifying client identity and age
  • Evaluating treatment suitability and potential risks
  • Reviewing relevant health information
  • Maintaining accurate treatment and consent records
  • Processing booking fees and service payments
  • Sending appointment reminders and preparation instructions
  • Providing aftercare guidance and follow-up communication
  • Responding to client questions and concerns
  • Meeting applicable licensing, inspection, legal, and recordkeeping requirements
  • Using promotional photographs or videos only with separate authorization

Sensitive information is used only for purposes reasonably necessary to provide services and meet applicable obligations.

3Government-Issued Identification

For client safety, identity verification, and applicable legal compliance, we may require a valid government-issued photo ID before your appointment.

Identification may be securely uploaded through our client portal.

Your identification will never be intentionally displayed publicly, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated marketing purposes.

Access is restricted to authorized individuals and service providers with a legitimate business need.

You may also be asked to present your original identification when arriving for your appointment.

4Health Information & Client Records

Your health and treatment information helps us determine whether a procedure is appropriate and supports safe service delivery.

All medical questionnaires, treatment records, and consent documentation are treated as confidential.

Information may be shared with authorized service providers, regulatory authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes or required by law.

Ravaged Rose PMU operates as a cosmetic tattoo and permanent makeup business and may not qualify as a covered entity under the federal Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

Regardless of HIPAA applicability, we recognize the sensitivity of health-related information and take reasonable measures to protect it.

5Photography & Video Consent

Photography may be used to document procedures, track healing, and maintain accurate treatment records.

Public or promotional use of your images is entirely optional and requires separate authorization.

Clients may select one of the following preferences:

Full Image Authorization

Allows approved photographs or videos to be used for promotional purposes, including images showing identifiable facial features.

Cropped Image Authorization

Allows approved photographs or videos limited to the treatment area without intentionally showing the client's full face.

No Public Use

Your photographs and videos will not be used for public advertising, social media, or promotional purposes.

Declining promotional photography will not affect your eligibility to receive services.

You may contact us to withdraw permission for future promotional use. We will honor applicable requests and make reasonable efforts to remove content under our control. Previously shared or distributed content may not always be fully retrievable.

6Electronic Forms & Digital Signatures

To make your experience more convenient, our website may offer electronic intake forms, health questionnaires, identification uploads, and digital consent documents.

Completed forms and electronic signatures may be securely maintained as part of your client record.

These records may include submission dates, verification details, and appointment-related information.

Electronic records help us document consent, maintain accurate treatment histories, and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

7Information Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell client medical information, identification documents, or private treatment records.

To operate our business, we may work with trusted third-party providers, including:

  • Website hosting and secure storage services
  • Appointment scheduling and client management platforms
  • Electronic forms and signature providers
  • Payment processors and financing companies
  • Authorized business personnel
  • Government agencies or regulatory authorities when required by law

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

We do not authorize service providers to use sensitive client records for unrelated advertising.

8Data Security & Protection

Protecting your personal information is an important part of our business operations.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information collected.

Depending on the services and technology in use, these protections may include:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Restricted access to sensitive records
  • Authentication and account security controls
  • Secure document storage
  • Controlled access to client information

Although we take reasonable precautions, no online platform or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

If a security incident occurs, we will evaluate our obligations and provide notifications when required by applicable law.

9Client Record Retention

We maintain client records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, document treatments, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Nevada body art regulations establish recordkeeping obligations for certain client and procedure records.

Sensitive information, including identification images, will not be retained longer than necessary for legitimate business purposes or applicable legal requirements.

When records are no longer needed, reasonable steps will be taken to securely delete, destroy, or de-identify them, subject to legal and technical limitations.

10Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support:

  • Website functionality and performance
  • Appointment booking
  • Security features
  • General visitor analytics
  • Improvements to the online experience

Depending on the tools installed, analytics services may collect information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general website activity.

Optional advertising or tracking technologies will be disclosed and managed in accordance with applicable law.

11Your Privacy Rights

We respect your right to ask questions about your personal information.

You may contact us to request:

  • Access to personal information maintained about you
  • Correction of inaccurate personal or contact details
  • Updates to your communication preferences
  • Withdrawal of optional marketing or photography consent
  • Deletion of eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations

Identity verification may be required before certain requests can be completed.

Some information must be retained when required by law or necessary for legitimate legal purposes.

12Minors & Age Requirements

Our website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization.

Permanent makeup and tattoo services are subject to applicable age restrictions and identification requirements.

Any services involving minors, where legally permitted, must comply with Nevada law and applicable local health authority regulations.

13External Websites & Payment Providers

Our website may include links or integrations with independent services, including booking platforms, payment processors, financing providers, and social media websites.

These third-party services maintain their own privacy policies and terms of use.

We encourage clients to review those policies before sharing personal or financial information.

Ravaged Rose PMU is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party websites.

14Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, website technology, business practices, or legal requirements change.

The most current version will be available on our website and will display its effective date.

We encourage clients to review this page periodically.
